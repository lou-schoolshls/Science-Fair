This is a science fair project for students at SHLS.\
We are working to make 3D printing as safe as possible, especially with non-enclosed printers.\
This project started August 20, 2026.

We are working to make 3D printing as safe as possible for everyone. Especially people with non-enclosure printers.\
We are looking to make this the cheapest possible product for safe and efficient 3D printing.\
This is not only for a science fair project, but to make the future of innovators and developers safer and cleaner.

We are trying very hard on this; all of us have experience with fumes and microplastics from 3D prints, and we know how harmful they can be.\
Science isn't just about making discoveries, but also helping the people who may need it.\
We are doing this project to benefit hobbyists, developers, and commercial users.\
That's our goal with this project.
